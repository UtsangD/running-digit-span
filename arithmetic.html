Working Memory - Mental Calculation

Instructions

You will hear short arithmetic word problems spoken aloud. Solve each problem mentally.

Do not use paper, a calculator, or another device. Enter only the numeric answer.

Each item has a 30-second limit. You may repeat the question once if the audio was not clear.

Format

The session uses 22 randomized items selected from easy through semi-hard tiers.

Items are ordered from easier to harder, but the exact questions vary by session.

This is a WAIS-inspired practice/norming task, not an official WAIS administration.
